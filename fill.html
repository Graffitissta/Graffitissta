Company Presentation & Product Booklet

It was a pleasure to design the company presentation for Fill-eco and the product booklet for Pugedon, in collaboration with Devocean. With its strong ecological values, Fill-eco wanted a design that reflected sustainability and clarity. We used the brand’s color palette and a minimal, modern aesthetic with clean layouts and balanced typography. The Pugedon booklet — presenting a device that dispenses food and water for stray animals each time someone recycles — followed the same visual logic, ensuring a cohesive and eco-conscious identity.
