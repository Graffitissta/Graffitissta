The second book I’m presenting was created for my Illustration class. We were asked to choose a poem or song and illustrate it, and I selected “O Mavros Gatos” (The Black Cat), a song by Vasilis Papakonstantinou. It was a project I truly enjoyed, inspired by vintage cartoon aesthetics. All the illustrations were created by me in Adobe Illustrator.

Illustration Project —Reflections of Culture in
the National Garden

The last book I’m presenting was created for the course Illustration of a Literary Book. The theme was “Reflections of Culture in the National Garden.” In this project, I explored the relationship between past and future, combining elements that reference both time periods. Some parts were illustrated in black and white to evoke a sense of history and nostalgia, while others featured more vibrant colors to represent modernity and change. The result was a visual dialogue between tradition and evolution within the same cultural space.
