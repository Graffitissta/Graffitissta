Logo Proposal

In May 2025, in collaboration with Devocean, I designed the logo for a physiotherapy center. The logo was officially registered and became the foundation for the overall brand identity. As part of the project, I also created business cards, envelopes, and letterheads, ensuring a consistent and professional visual language.

Website Proposal

Following the branding work, I developed a website design proposal for the client. The goal was to highlight the center’s professionalism while providing a clear and user-friendly navigation experience. The design focused on clarity, simplicity, and trustworthiness, reflecting the values and nature of the physiotherapy services.
