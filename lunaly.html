Lunaly Brand Design

Lunaly is a personal concept project I designed from scratch as an experimental exploration. I created the brand identity, visual language, focusing on calm and clarity. Also all the photos of the jewelrys were created with AI.
Lunaly is a handcrafted jewelry brand that redefines accessories as personal talismans. Combining premium sterling silver with symbolic design, the collection offers modern women 'charms' for luck and balance. Each piece is more than an ornament; it’s a wearable intention for positive energy and inner harmony.

Site Design

Designed in Figma, the site focuses on intuitive navigation and storytelling. Users can seamlessly browse the collection, filter by jewelry type (Necklaces, Bracelets, Rings), and explore the unique energetic properties and 'powers' of each talisman before purchasing. The interface balances luxury aesthetics with a smooth, conversion-oriented shopping journey.
