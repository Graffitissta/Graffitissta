GTM Normaxis Redesign

This was a freelance project I completed in September 2025 for Devocean. The client provided the homepage as a starting point and asked me to continue designing the remaining pages, including About Us, Services, and Contact. My goal was to extend the existing visual style while ensuring a smooth and cohesive user experience across the site. I focused on maintaining consistency, improving layout and interactions, and finding creative solutions that respected the established design framework. This project allowed me to practice problem-solving within constraints while refining my UI/UX skills.

Normaxis Gloves Redesign Proposal

Following my collaboration on the GTM Normaxis project, I worked on a redesign concept for the Normaxis Gloves website. I followed a similar aesthetic to maintain brand consistency while exploring ways to modernize the layout and highlight the products’ quality. The focus was on creating a clean, user-focused design that enhances usability and visual appeal. This project allowed me to extend the brand’s visual language and experiment with new design directions while keeping a cohesive look across related platforms.
